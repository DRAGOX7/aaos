# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students using AAOS as a trusted academic workspace. They inspect courses,
assignments, and documents; ask grounded questions over academic materials;
review tasks and approvals; and understand activity and system state.

## Product Purpose

AAOS is a local-first Academic AI Operating System. It helps students
organize academic work while preserving provenance, trustworthy answers,
human control, and explicit authority boundaries.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, premium, intelligent, and trustworthy. Ask AAOS is the core
intelligence layer, while tasks, approvals, health, and citations communicate
operational truth without overstating capability.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon palettes, excessive gradients, heavy glassmorphism, decorative
clutter, over-animation, generic admin-dashboard density, fake SaaS metrics,
invented schedules or health state, and any UI that obscures provenance or
human control.

## Design Principles

- Make grounded academic context visible and easy to judge.
- Prefer truthful empty and unavailable states over invented completeness.
- Give human decisions and authority boundaries clear visual weight.
- Use restrained depth, calm hierarchy, and familiar product affordances.
- Keep the interface useful, accessible, responsive, and locally trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve strong contrast, semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus
states, responsive layouts, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion
behavior. Status meaning must not rely on color alone.
